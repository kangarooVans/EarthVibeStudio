ABOUT THE STUDIO

A Space Built for Sound

Earth Vibe Studio is a professional recording and music production studio based in Piliyandala, Sri Lanka. We provide a creative environment where artists, musicians, creators, and brands can bring their ideas to life.

By integrating acoustic excellence with modern production setups, our multi-disciplinary approach covers everything from initial acoustic composition to full-scale visual post-production.
